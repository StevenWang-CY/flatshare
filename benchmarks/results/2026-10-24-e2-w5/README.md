# E2 records of W5's re-measure (2026-10-24)

The compact records of the points W5 measured after its fixes to make-room and to E2's harness ([E2](../../../docs/EXPERIMENTS.md#e2-voice-under-agent-load)), as [W4's records](../2026-10-23-e2) keep each point: `run.json`, `arm.toml`, `engine.json`, `summary.json` and the callers' `bots/summary.json` and `bots/turns.jsonl`. `make reproduce-e2-w5` regenerates W5's report from them into `build/reproduce-e2-w5` (`E2_W5_REPORT=DIR` to choose). [`report/`](report) holds the report these records give, every file of it ([`frontier.md`](report/frontier.md) for the tables), as [W4's](../2026-10-23-e2/report) does: `make reproduce-e2-w5 E2_W5_REPORT=benchmarks/results/2026-10-24-e2-w5/report` rewrites it, and the bench's tests fail while it is not what the records give. They are kept apart from W4's so that W5's report reads its own voice-alone runs for the SLO, and because W5's harness measures differently: each call opens with an unmeasured warm-up turn (`"warmup": true` in `bots/turns.jsonl`), the agents' goodput is also counted over the calls' first 240 s (`background.fixed_window`), each point checked the engine's scheduler class before it started, and each record names every GPU node's deployed revision (`versions.gpu_source`). Their `versions.flatshare_bench` is 0.1.0, as in W4's records: cyan's editable install kept its metadata from before the packages' 0.3.0 version, while the code it ran is the revision in `versions.source`.

72 points, all on vLLM 0.32.0 and Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507-FP8 on Phastform's RTX 5060 Ti, with Phastform at v0.3-71-g66deebb (W5's campaign and the PR campaign) and v0.3-90-gd43b46d (the extension and calls per GPU), in the campaigns of [`benchmarks/e2`](../../e2/README.md):

| Campaign | Points | What |
|---|---:|---|
| [`w5-r1`](w5-r1) to [`w5-r5`](w5-r5) | 4 each | Voice alone, the frozen box, and the PRs' 48-token configuration on make-room as W5's fixes leave it and as v0.3 built it (the control, `make-room-v03`) |
| [`w5-pr-r1`](w5-pr-r1) to [`w5-pr-r5`](w5-pr-r5) | 4 each | The frozen box, the KV preemption alone (#63350), and both PRs at 16 and at 48 tokens, on the fixed make-room |
| [`w5-ext-r1`](w5-ext-r1) to [`w5-ext-r5`](w5-ext-r5) | 4 each | Preempt-resubmit and the PRs' 48-token configuration in the discriminating scenario, and the frozen box and the PRs' 48-token configuration in the standard scenario (16 agent sessions of the whole corpus) |
| [`w5-cap-r1`](w5-cap-r1) to [`w5-cap-r3`](w5-cap-r3) | 4 each | Calls per GPU: voice alone and the PRs' 48-token configuration with 4 and with 6 callers |

Round N of each campaign replays the same sample of the agents' traces (`agents-seed` 10 + N) and rotates the order of its configurations, or of its engine groups, from round to round. The PRs' rows are the `make-room` variant on vLLM 0.32.0 with the PRs' switch values, as W5 built it: it counted a request's first tokens from the outputs processed, so under the box's asynchronous scheduling the budget cap held a step past its window, which only a reply of at least the window's tokens meets ([after W5](../../../docs/BENCHMARKS.md#after-w5-the-first-tokens-in-flight)).

The calls per GPU rounds differ in how a point's callers joined their rooms. `w5-cap-r1`, like every campaign before it, created them all at once. In `w5-cap-r2`'s first run, LiveKit's dispatcher dropped the agent job of a room created within milliseconds of the others ("no workers with sufficient capacity", 2026-10-25 08:38 and 08:44 UTC, its first point twice) and does not retry, so that room's caller waited for an agent that never came; its round was run again after `w5-cap-r3`. From 08:46:20 UTC cyan's callers joined their rooms a quarter second apart (`JOIN_STAGGER_S` in `flatshare-voice bots`), which `w5-cap-r3` and `w5-cap-r2` ran with: the voice worker received their rooms' job requests 236–268 ms apart, against 0–11 ms in `w5-cap-r1`. The stagger is at most 1.25 s, with six callers, within the warm-up turn every call opens with, and the callers' think times (1–3 s a turn) set the calls' turns apart from then on.

`compare.py` gives BENCHMARKS' W5 tables from these records: each configuration's median and range by campaign, the SLO by the number of callers and the calls per GPU against it round by round, the configurations several campaigns ran pooled over them, the differences round by round, and E3's table. E3 also reads `e3.tsv`: for each point with agents, the engine's steps over the calls and make-room's own counts over them (the steps in which the budget cap held lower-priority prefill back, the prefill tokens and admissions it held back in them, and its preemptions), which the compact records do not keep. `e3_counters.py` wrote it from the raw run directories' metric snapshots and Phastform's engine journal. The raw records, which the report does not need (the gateway's request log, AIPerf's exports, the metric snapshots, the voice worker's journal, the GPU's samples and the tools' logs), are not committed: they are kept for the v1.0 release's assets.
